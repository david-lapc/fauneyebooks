A little spark of creativity

Fun & Free

Take a tiny story break with this no-prep imagination game. All you need is a pencil, some paper, and a curious mind.

Creative prompt

Invent a forest friend

Imagine a new friend who lives in a woodland clearing. Draw them, then fill in the blanks:

  1. My forest friend is called
  2. They are special because
  3. Their favorite thing to do is
  4. One day, they discover
  5. They ask a friend to help by

Make up your own ending, or tell your story to someone you love.

Tip: use your browser’s Print command to print this activity.
